## Getting Started

<Steps>
  <Step title="Create your account">
    Register at the Mydva portal with your full name, email, and password. Your account is protected with JWT-based authentication.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Take the Dosha Quiz">
    Answer questions about your body type, digestion, sleep, and lifestyle. The AI engine computes your Vata, Pitta, and Kapha percentages in seconds.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Receive your wellness plan">
    The Groq Llama 3.3-70B model generates a personalized plan covering diet, herbs, exercise, and daily routines tailored to your Prakriti.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Connect with an expert">
    Browse verified Ayurvedic practitioners, book a time slot, and join a live video consultation — all without leaving the platform.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
  Mydva is a wellness guidance tool. Always consult a qualified healthcare professional before starting any new treatment or supplement regimen.
</Note>
