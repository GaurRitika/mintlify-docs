Mydva (AyurGuide) brings five thousand years of Ayurvedic science into your daily wellness routine. Instead of one-size-fits-all health advice, you receive a personalised picture of your unique biological constitution — your **Prakriti** — and a complete toolkit to act on it. From an AI-powered Dosha quiz to interactive 3D visualisations of medicinal herbs and chakra energy systems, right through to live video calls with certified Ayurvedic practitioners, every feature on the platform is designed around *you*.

## What Mydva Is

Mydva is a full-stack digital health platform that digitises the traditional Ayurvedic framework and supercharges it with modern technology. At its core, the platform analyses your physical characteristics, digestion patterns, sleep quality, and mental tendencies to compute your exact **Vata / Pitta / Kapha** percentage distribution. It then uses that profile to generate bespoke dietary plans, herbal recommendations, and daily routine guidance — all powered by a state-of-the-art large language model.

Beyond analysis, Mydva gives you an immersive environment to *learn*: explore medicinal plants rendered in interactive 3D, journey through the seven chakra energy centres, and discover Ayurvedic wellness centres near you. When you need a human expert, the built-in telehealth module lets you book and attend a live video consultation with a verified Ayurvedic doctor without ever leaving the platform.

## The Three Pillars

### 1 · AI Dosha Analysis

The Dosha engine processes a multi-dimensional questionnaire covering physiology, digestion, sleep, and emotional patterns. A dynamic scoring algorithm returns precise percentage breakdowns for each of the three doshas — Vata, Pitta, and Kapha — and identifies your primary and secondary constitution. The Groq Llama 3.3 (70B) model then uses those results, combined with your personal health history and current symptoms, to generate a fully tailored Ayurvedic health strategy.

### 2 · 3D Herbal & Chakra Exploration

An interactive 3D herbal remedies explorer, built with React Three Fiber and Three.js, lets you examine medicinal plants in detail — including botanical names, active constituents, doshic impacts, and traditional preparation methods. A separate 3D chakra system guides you through each of the seven energy centres with meditation assistance, mantra audio, mudra visualisations, and breathing rhythm guides.

### 3 · Live Expert Video Consultations

Mydva's telehealth module connects you with certified Ayurvedic doctors for real-time video consultations via WebRTC. You can browse practitioner availability, book appointment slots, and attend sessions — all within the platform. Medical records and prescriptions are stored centrally so your care history is always at hand.
